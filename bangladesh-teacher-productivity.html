Working paper

Enhancing teacher productivity with information on students and skills: Experimental evidence from Bangladesh

Alejandro J. Ganimian

with S. Djaker, N. Angrist & S. Sabarwal

Teacher development Impact evaluation Bangladesh
Latest manuscript ↗
Overview Instruments

Overview

  • In 312 Bangladeshi schools, grade-6 math teachers received reports identifying students and topics needing support, plus an annotated answer key.
  • The program raised math achievement by 0.23 standard deviations and reading by 0.17, with similar gains across the baseline achievement distribution.
  • At $5.06 per student, the program helped teachers distribute interactions more equitably and emphasize more foundational content.
